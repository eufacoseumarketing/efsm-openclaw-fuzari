# KPI patterns

Use these patterns to avoid shallow analysis.

## Volume metrics
- impressions
- clicks
- sessions
- leads
- opportunities
- sales

## Efficiency metrics
- CTR
- CPC
- CPM
- CPL
- CPA
- CAC

## Rate metrics
- CVR
- lead-to-opportunity rate
- opportunity-to-sale rate
- close rate

## Framing rule
Never present efficiency without volume, and never present volume without business context.
