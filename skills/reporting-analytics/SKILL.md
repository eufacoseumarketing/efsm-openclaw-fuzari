# Reporting Analytics

Use this skill when the request is primarily about understanding performance, not just formatting data.

## Good use cases
- campaign reporting
- sales reporting
- lead funnel analysis
- weekly or monthly business reviews
- KPI summaries
- budget pacing checks
- trend analysis
- anomaly detection
- metric interpretation with recommendations

## Workflow
1. Clarify audience and decision context.
2. Identify the source of truth and reporting window.
3. Separate facts, interpretations, and recommendations.
4. Surface the top findings first.
5. Add only the supporting metrics needed to defend the conclusion.
6. End with actions, risks, or follow-ups when useful.

## Agency defaults
Optimize for:
- signal over noise
- decision usefulness
- scannability
- clear comparisons
- explicit recommendations

## Output shapes
Choose the right output:
- chat summary for quick decisions
- doc/deck for executive sharing
- spreadsheet when detailed auditability matters
- PDF only when packaging is the main goal
