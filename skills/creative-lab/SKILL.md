# Creative Lab

Use this skill for exploratory creative work that sits outside the normal path of proposals, decks, reports, and business documents.

## Good use cases
- generative or algorithmic visuals
- aesthetic experiments
- unusual creative concepts
- niche visual assets
- playful internal creative exploration

## Guardrails
- experimental output should still be intentional
- do not confuse exploratory art with standard business deliverables
- if the user needs a commercially usable artifact, switch to `marketing-materials`
