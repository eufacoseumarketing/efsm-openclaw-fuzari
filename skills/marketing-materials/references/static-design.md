Adapted from imported canvas-design and related visual skills.

Use for static marketing and sales materials such as one-pagers, posters, proposal visuals, report covers, and simple campaign concepts.

## Principles
- clarity before ornament
- hierarchy before decoration
- composition before detail
- commercial usefulness before gallery-piece ambition

## Build sequence
1. Identify objective and audience.
2. Reduce the message to the few ideas that matter.
3. Choose a composition that supports that message.
4. Apply typography, color, and imagery as reinforcement.
5. Leave breathing room.

## Visual quality bar
- strong headline hierarchy
- clear scan path
- disciplined palette
- proper margins
- no accidental overlaps
- no filler text pretending to be design
