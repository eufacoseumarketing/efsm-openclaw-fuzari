# Client Reporting

Use this skill for reports meant for clients, not just internal teams.

## Core rule
Translate performance into client-understandable business meaning.

A good client report answers:
- what happened
- why it happened
- what matters
- what we recommend next

## Workflow
1. Identify the client audience and maturity level.
2. Summarize headline outcomes first.
3. Explain movement in plain language.
4. Be honest about problems without sounding chaotic.
5. Recommend concrete next actions.
6. Avoid exposing unnecessary internal noise.

## Tone
- confident
- clear
- factual
- not defensive
- not jargon-heavy unless the client expects it

## Guardrails
- Do not dump raw metrics without interpretation.
- Do not hide bad news.
- Do not overstate weak wins.
- Do not promise certainty where the data is thin.
